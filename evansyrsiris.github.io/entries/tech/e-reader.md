# You should get an E-reader.
date: 17/10/2026

My thoughts on a Kindle 4th gen.

## **What even is an E-reader?**

The stand out quality of an E-reader is the use of an E-ink screen.
An E-ink screen, differs from your traditional LCD screen, as instead of its own continous backlight, it uses reflection of ambiet lighting, giving it a much more paper-like feel. Not to mention, it uses tiny microcapsules that are either black or white, corresponding to a negative or positive charge, and thus can be moved around using a small current.

E-ink, also only uses power when the image is changed. A culmination of all this, is that the refresh rate is really, really low, making it quite unfit for animations or fast changing images, leaving it in the very niche category, of a book reading device.

E-readers, are typically pretty expensive (Even in the 400 euro category..) making an already niche item, even more niche. 

## **How I got my hands on one.**

While I was browsing Vendora for a ThinkPad (more to come soon) I came across a Kindle 4th Gen, for only 30 euro. Now, a Kindle 4th gen is a 2011 device, making it severely underpowered, even for the time. However, I was really amazed by its design, and with a healthy curiosity for E-ink, I bought it.

When it arrived, one of the obvious things is the stark design and how..almost industrial it looks.
Like, really, look at this thing

Truly, a design language that is long past us..for better or worse. Now, the device has a small scratch on the screen, but it did not really create a problem.

I was already pretty excited about it, despite not even having my own books on it yet.

## **The port from hell.**

It charges, with a MicroUSB which is quite honestly, awful. For me, it was really difficult to find a MicroUSB that could do data transfer, and even when I did, it could only stay for 10 minutes, probably cause the cable was so awful. This, obviously needed a fix. I did not want to get a new one..which..gets us to..

## **Why you should get one.**
Jailbreaking. You can jailbreak it. Even if you dont read that actively, jailbreaking it, is so fun and is sure to expand your knowledge of tech. The specifics will depend on your device, but for me, it was really cool, having to run all sorts of exploits under a time limit..as it would, well, disconnect otherwise. Eventually however, after I managed to install a launcher/app popular in the community, called KOReader, I got access to a terminal. Which, is great, as it gave me access to what we were truly after, setting up an SSH session (Which, is quite insane to think about running it on here, there is always something really satisfying about breaking open a locked down system) which gives us access, ofcourse to SFTP, allowing us to finally transfer files without a time limit. 

## **What I've been using it for.**
Its been a few days, and I've been mainly using it to read manga. Despite the small screen, I find it to not be that tiring, even less so than my phone I'd say, though on some specific text it can get a bit annoying. I've also had a great time customising KOReader, and the fact that it can always stay on cause a fixed image does not draw power, allows you to set a custom screensaver, again, after you jailbreak it.

## **The future / closing thoughts.**
I definently wanna keep using it for the foresable future, as I think its also gonna be a great oppurtunity for me to read more, as literature is a really important hobby one should take up, in my opinion. Furthermore, I want to perhaps experiment with changing its storage, and maybe even replacing the screen, though from what I've gathered this is quite a complex and even..a bit expensive (20 euro, still about 80% of the price I got it for) electronics project, so that can wait.
