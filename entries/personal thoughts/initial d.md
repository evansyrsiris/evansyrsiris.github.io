# Initial D: The best anime you should NOT watch. 
date: 27/10/2026

Initial D is a strange case.

## Synopsis
Initial D follows a senior high school student in Japan, Takumi Fujiwara. Takumi initially is really uninterested in life. He goes to school, anddoes delivery trough an old Toyota AE-86 for his Dad's tofu buisiness who used to be a legendary street racer, secretly training him. Trough his best friend, Itsuki, he gets involved in the world of underground street racing and accidentally becomes a racer when an opponent who has come to challenge the local street racing team, Keisuke, is beaten by Takumi who turns out is a beast behind the wheel.

## Philosophical themes
Funnliy enough, Initial D is quite deep in what it tries to approach. That being, dreams. And finding what you enjoy to do in life, despite insecurity. Takumi begins enjoying racing. A lot. He is even bummed out at one point for not having his own car, so he thinks he cant be a street racer (The 86 belongs to his father). In races, he often doubts himself, and the first time he loses, he finally cries, really showing how far he has come. But despite all that, he never gives up. Always comes back, adapting to his opponents and eventually winning. We also see him, finally putting in the work, and throwing himself into racing when things like his relationship are going really badly. Its a show about not giving up your dreams. Really its a classic story that is still..kind off beatiful, honestly.

## Why you should not watch it 
The animation, even for the time..is bad. Its terrible, infact. Despite that, while the core message is good as discussed above..the show struggles with a lot of filler, and like a lot of anime, some really weird scenes. The writing also often boils down to "Oh no! Takumi is losing! How is he going to win this time?! Oh look! He did it again!". The character development outside of Takumi himself and a select few, is also quite lacking. Characters often appear as just..commentary, or have a really shallow arc before disappearing again. 

## What makes it one of the best
I dont think there is a single show that can replicate the hype one feels when watching the racing scenes. I honestly think all the budget went there. Like, for the quality of the show they go UNREASONABLY hard. Also, as discussed, when the show wants to, it pulls some insane writing moments..it just often does not want to. Finally...you cant mention Initial D without mentioning the music. The Eurobeat genre has become synonymous with it, and for good reason too. It features really strong tracks, like aside the obvious Running in the 90s, GAS GAS GAS and Deja Vu, you get bangers like, love & money, Spaceboy, Back On The Rocks and Night Trip. 

## Summary
Initial D is a show that has some really deep themes hidden behind quite bad writing, and also features an uncanny ability to cause insane hype for its important scenes, that not even 10/10 shows can replicate.
